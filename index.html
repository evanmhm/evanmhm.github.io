About Me

My name is Evan Hopper-Moore, and I am a programmer, web designer, musician, and aspiring artist. This website serves as a kind of online resume for me.

In freshman year of High School I learned Java and Python, while also working on solo projects in Unity 3D. Throughout High School I learned more and more about coding and I really enjoyed working on projects with other students and solving coding challenges.

I am currently a second year student at Oregon State University, majoring in Computer Science, and thouroughly enjoying my studies. In my free time I enjoy playing music with friends, working on small graphic design projects, and programmming generative and reactive visual art with Processing.
