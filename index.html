About Me

My name is Evan Hopper-Moore and I am a programmer, web designer, musician, and wanna-be artist. This website serves as a kind of online resume for me.

I started coding in middle school for personal projects, making small games that usually amounted to nothing. It wasn't until I joined the South Eugene Robotics Team that I really started to get into coding. In freshman year of High School I learned Java and Python, while also working on solo projects in Unity 3D. Throughout High School I learned more and more about coding and how much I enjoyed working on projects and solving coding puzzles.

I am currently a first year student at Oregon State University, majoring in Computer Science, and thouroughly enjoying my studies. In my free time I enjoy playing music with friends, working on small graphic design projects, and programmming generative visual art with Processing.
